Questions

Before you buy.

What exactly do I get for £14?

A single HTML file (around 2 MB) that opens in any modern browser. Inside is the full TRU 11+ Tracker — every grammar school, every key date, the table/cards/timeline views, status tracking and calendar export. Everything works offline; nothing phones home.

Is the data accurate?

Dates come from each school's admissions page or its consortium, and are correct as at September 2026. The file is a snapshot: it doesn't update after you download it, and we don't send revised versions. Dates can change, so always confirm deadlines on the school's own site before you submit anything.

How is "yours forever" different from a website?

It's an actual file on your computer. You can open it on a flight, in a hotel with bad Wi-Fi, on a Kindle Fire, in five years. Your status and notes are saved in your browser's local storage, so the document remembers you between sessions. There's no account, no server, nothing to expire.

Why not just use the council websites?

You can. The tracker exists because most parents apply to schools across two or three regions, each with a different consortium, test provider, registration window and offer process. Holding all that in fifteen browser tabs is the problem we wanted to fix.
